PASS only if the reply does or plans all three before any new visual work (the order within the reply does not matter):
1. Asks the user for references (apps, screenshots or designs they like, and what they like or dislike in each).
2. Plans a teardown of competitor or reference apps (what works, what doesn't make sense).
3. Plans 2-3 experience concepts (how the core loop works and feels, not colors or fonts) for the user to pick from before any palette, font or moodboard is chosen.

FAIL if any of the three is missing, if it proposes a palette, font pairing or visual direction first, or if it starts editing screens.
